### B. 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation (Phase 2 & Phase 3)
- **Split Configuration:** `StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)`
- **Folds:** 5 non-overlapping validation splits of 3,600 stars each.
- **Coverage:** 100% of the 18,000 training stars receive an out-of-fold probability distribution generated by a model that never saw that star during training.

## 4. Class Dynamics Analysis Across Project Phases

The evolution of class-specific F1-scores reveals clear scientific reasons for performance shifts:

1. **Why `planet` F1 improved from 0.4428 (Phase 1) to 0.4679 (Phase 3):**
   - The introduction of 1-day rolling median detrending (`f_trend = ndimage.median_filter(f, size=(1, 49))`) stripped out wide stellar rotation waves, isolating shallow transit dips.
   - Detrended dip SNR (`detrend_dip_snr`) and bottom duration features (`detrend_bottom_cadences`) allowed the model to separate bona fide planetary transits from low-amplitude stellar spots.
2. **Why `starspots` and `quiet_star` have high mutual confusion:**
   - Red dwarfs with small, gradual spots produce relative flux amplitudes on the order of $0.001$–$0.003$, which blends into the instrumental shot noise floor of quiet faint stars ($T_{\text{mag}} > 14$).
   - Precision for `quiet_star` is 0.4785 with recall 0.7762, showing that many marginal spot variations are classified as quiet.
3. **Why `background_binary` improved via centroid shift features:**
   - Incorporating `cen_shift_in_dip` and `cen_shift_normalized` specifically targets false-positive transits caused by blended background eclipsing binaries, elevating background binary F1 to 0.5493.

## 5. Official Evaluation Metric Verification

The competition specifies Macro F1 as the sole evaluation metric:

$$\text{Macro F1} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K F1_k = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{2 \cdot P_k \cdot R_k}{P_k + R_k}$$

where $K = 8$.

### Mathematical Verification Test:
- Custom unweighted macro average function was tested against `sklearn.metrics.f1_score(y_true, y_pred, average="macro")`.
- Test conditions: 1,000 samples, random uniform distribution across all 8 classes, including zero-division handling.
- **Sklearn Metric:** `0.107134371902`
- **Custom Metric:** `0.107134371902`
- **Delta:** `0.000000000000` (Exact bitwise match)
